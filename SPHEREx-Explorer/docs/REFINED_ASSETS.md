# Refined astronomy asset provenance

Historical category-asset pass, retrieved and inspected on 2026-10-15. The current homepage uses a data-derived sky sphere; Earth and Jupiter textures are unused historical assets. Full profiles now use the exact spreadsheet-linked assets (`CANDIDATE_IMAGES.md`). Files are in `public/assets/refined/`. All seven images were downloaded from official NASA asset servers; ownership and reuse terms remain those below. These are contextual astronomy images, not observations of the synthetic workbook records and not SPHEREx data products. No generated imagery was added in this asset pass.

All downloaded images preserve the published orientation, aspect ratio, composition, and color treatment. Changes are limited to resizing and JPEG/WebP encoding. No objects, labels, or scientific features were painted in or removed. The source's `Artist’s Concept` label remains in `centaur.webp`.
